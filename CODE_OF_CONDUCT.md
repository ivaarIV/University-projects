# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This repository supports a respectful, constructive learning environment. Participants should communicate professionally and treat one another with respect.

## Expected behavior

- Review code, not a person's worth or ability.
- Give constructive, specific, respectful feedback.
- Ask questions in good faith and be open to clarification.
- Respect privacy, academic-integrity rules, and intellectual-property rights.
- Do not harass, discriminate against, or intimidate other participants.

## Unacceptable behavior

Personal attacks, harassment, discriminatory language, sharing private information, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the course staff or designated instructor: [add official contact method]. Do not publish sensitive details in public issues.
